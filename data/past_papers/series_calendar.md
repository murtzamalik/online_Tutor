# Series calendar (how to enumerate what exists)
| Track | Typical series | How to enumerate |
|---|---|---|
| GCSE AQA / Edexcel / OCR / Eduqas / WJEC | June (main). Nov resit series exist for some boards (English/maths mostly). | Each board's "past papers" page for the spec; download paper + mark scheme + examiner report. |
| iGCSE Edexcel 4CH1 | Jan, May/Jun, Oct/Nov | Pearson International GCSE past-papers page. |
| iGCSE Cambridge 0620/0971 | Feb/Mar, May/Jun, Oct/Nov (variants per zone) | Cambridge past-papers pages (subject page → past papers). |
Always also ingest the board's **specimen / sample assessment materials** (`series = SPEC`). Record `series` as `JUN2023`, `NOV2022`, `SPEC`.
