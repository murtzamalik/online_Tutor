Study Loop — what has been built, and how it looks

A visual status of the working web product — marketing site, student learning loop, parent controls, staff tools — plus a plain-English explanation of how AI is used and which providers power the pilot. Screenshots from the live local demo build.

1. In short

Study Loop is running as a full web application. Families can sign in, learners practise with instant marking and an AI tutor, parents oversee progress and privacy, and staff can review content and A-Level marking queues. The look and feel is calm, modern, and education-focused — navy brand mark, clear typography, and role-specific shells so students, parents and staff each see a focused workspace.

4. How we use AI (plain-English guide)

Think of Study Loop’s AI as a carefully supervised teaching assistant — not a chatbot that freely invents answers. Every AI request goes through our own AI Gateway: a secure door that checks safety, hides personal details, picks the right model for the job, and keeps a record for quality and cost control. Children’s names, school details and contact data are not sent to AI vendors.

Which AI providers we use (pilot stack)

ProviderIn simple termsWhat we use it for
Google Gemini (Flash) A fast, capable general AI from Google Everyday tutoring help and many marking / feedback tasks where speed matters
Groq (open models) Very fast hosting of large open-source models Heavier reasoning when we need a stronger second opinion or bigger model
Ollama (on our machine) AI that can run locally as a backup Fallback if a cloud provider is slow or unavailable — keeps the demo resilient
Anthropic Claude (optional) A well-known paid AI, kept as a switchable option Available as a rollback / alternative path if we choose that routing later

In practice these providers are reached through one control layer (LiteLLM), so Study Loop can change or add models without rebuilding the whole student app. Parents and students never pick a provider — the product chooses the right tool automatically.

Where AI helps in the product

Feature the family seesWhat the AI is doingBenefit
Instant marking Reads the student’s answer against the mark scheme and explains the marks Feedback in seconds instead of waiting for a teacher — with reasons, not just a tick or cross
AI tutor chat Talks the learner through a stuck question (guided help, with safety checks) Support anytime at home; encourages thinking instead of only dumping the final answer
Safety checks Screens learner messages and tutor replies for risk (e.g. safeguarding concerns) Safer conversations; serious issues can be stopped and escalated to humans
Question generation & QC Helps draft practice items; staff still approve before students see them Faster content coverage while humans stay in control of quality
Reports & insights (where enabled) Turns practice data into clearer parent-facing summaries Parents see progress in plain language, not raw scores only
A-Level review assist Gives a provisional AI mark; specialist staff can confirm or adjust Speed for the student, human oversight for high-stakes answers
Important for parents and schools: AI never replaces exam boards or teachers. It speeds up practice and feedback. Sensitive journeys (safeguarding, final A-Level release, content publish) stay under human control. Personal data is kept out of vendor prompts.

Simple picture of the flow

Student asks or submits an answer
→ Study Loop app (practice / tutor screen)
→ AI Gateway (safety + privacy filter + choose model)
→ Gemini / Groq / Ollama (the actual AI engine)
→ Answer comes back through the same Gateway
→ Student sees marking feedback or tutor reply

5. Suggested walkthrough (demo)

  1. Open the homepage and pricing to see positioning and plans.
  2. Parent login → dashboard → open a child profile (controls and privacy).
  3. Child login → Home → start Practice → submit an answer → open Tutor → ask a Chemistry question → browse Revise / Mock.
  4. Optional: staff login to show review or content QC.

6. What’s next (polish before full UAT)

Core journeys above are in place. Ahead of a formal UAT we are tightening:

  • Diagnostic question display (content clarity)
  • Marking edge cases on short answers
  • A few secondary student routes and legal copy depth
  • Voice tutor entry polish

None of these change the overall look-and-feel direction shown in this pack.
